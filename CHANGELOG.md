## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-07

First public release.

### Added
- Capture a region, a window (click it, or pick it from a list even when it's covered), one display or all displays, through a frozen per-monitor overlay with a magnifier. DPI-correct on mixed-DPI setups.
- Rectangles, squares, ovals, circles and splines (freehand or point by point, open or closed), all editable.
- Numbers and tags with five label styles (box, circle, corner tab, callout, halo text), nine anchor points, inside/on-edge/outside placement and readable colour schemes, including *Adaptive*. Numbers update live; formats 1/A/a/I/i with prefix and start value.
- Notes sidebar with a comment per area.
- **Expand** for handover: comments beside each area with leader lines, or a list on the right or below. **Copy** puts exactly what you see on the clipboard.
- Snapshot library with autosave, thumbnails, search, rename and delete (to the Recycle Bin), plus per-snapshot undo history for the session.
- Export to PNG (lossless, native resolution), JPEG and BMP, and to a `.snapnote` file; copy the notes as Markdown.
- Configurable system-wide shortcuts with conflict detection; tray icon; start with Windows.
- Nine themes and a custom title bar.
- Support tools: rolling logs with start-up timings, crash reports, a problem dialog, support bundles, and optional opt-in crash reporting via Sentry.
